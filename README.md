## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a Software Developer with over 15 years of experience in IT systems. My career foundation includes extensive work in technical support, network administration, and database management, which eventually evolved into a dedicated focus on software engineering. For the past 5 years, my core expertise has been building scalable, high-performance web applications.

* 💼 **Current Role:** Software Developer at Easy Pallet, developing systems to automate and manage distribution centers, including handling complex logistics and picking operations.
* 💻 **Freelance Work:** I also design and develop custom websites and systems as a freelancer, delivering tailored solutions using a diverse stack that includes Ruby on Rails, Laravel, React, Next.js, Vue.js, and WordPress.
* 🎓 **Education:** Finishing my Associate Degree in Systems Analysis and Development.
* 🚀 **What I'm building:** Currently developing a multi-tenant Fleet & Asset Management SaaS to control rented/loaned equipment, manage dispatch routes, and provide public-facing maintenance checklists. I am also the creator of **WhatsCLI** (a TypeScript command-line tool).
* 🤖 **Workflow:** Highly focused on AI-assisted development, actively integrating tools like Claude Code, Google Antigravity and Cursor to optimize daily coding, automate background tasks, and improve system architecture.
